The mechanism

This is a use-after-free at shutdown, and the core proves it rather than merely suggesting it.

The main thread was here at the moment of death:

QThreadPool::waitForDone            qthreadpool.cpp:849
QCoreApplication::~QCoreApplication qcoreapplication.cpp:939
main ()

In src/main.cpp the locals are declared app, backend, engine, so they destruct in reverse: engine, then backend, then app. Backend has no user-defined destructor, and QFutureWatcher's destructor does not wait for a running future — so Backend::m_store (a by-value GitStore member) was destroyed while the pool thread was still inside m_store.syncWithRemote(). The first and only thing that waits for that thread is ~QCoreApplication, one destructor too late.
